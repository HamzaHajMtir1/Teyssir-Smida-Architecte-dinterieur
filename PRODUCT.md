## Users

Primary visitors: potential clients, employers/recruiters, and academic or competition juries evaluating an interior architect’s work and background.

## Product Purpose

Personal portfolio website for Teyssir Smida, Architecte d’intérieur. It presents who she is, her projects (images and PDFs), and her professional path (experience and education). Success means a visitor can quickly understand her profile, browse her work, and contact her.

## Positioning

A personal practice portfolio — not a company marketing site. The site represents one designer: her voice, training, internships, and project work.

## Operating Context

Three primary routes:

- **Home** — introduce the person (name, title, short profile, path into projects/about/contact).
- **Projects** — gallery of projects with placeholder imagery until real photos/PDFs are supplied; each project can include images and a downloadable PDF.
- **About** — professional experiences, education, skills, certificates, and languages from the CV.
